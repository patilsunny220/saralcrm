Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 September 2026

This policy explains what the Saral CRM app does with information. It is written plainly, because a policy nobody can read protects nobody.

The app is used by businesses to keep track of their own customers. When a business uses it, that business decides what goes in. We hold it for them.

Who we are

Saral CRM is operated by Sunny Patil, India.

Email: patilsunny220@zohomail.in

For anything about your information (a question, a correction, or a request to delete it) write to that address.

What the app collects, and why

About you, the person using it

  • Your name, email address and phone number. So your colleagues know who you are, and so we can reach you about your account.
  • Your password, which is handled by Google Firebase Authentication. We never see it and cannot read it.
  • If you sign in with Google, Google gives the app your name and email address. Nothing else from your Google account is read.

About your customers

Whatever your business chooses to record: names, phone numbers, email addresses, what they are interested in, notes, stages, follow-up dates and the timeline of what has happened with them.

This is your business's information, not ours. We store it so the app works. We do not sell it, share it, or use it for advertising.

Your phone's contact list

If you allow it, the app reads the contacts on your phone so you can pick someone to add as a customer.

Your contact list is not uploaded. It is read on your phone and shown on your phone. Only the people you actually add as customers are saved, and only what the app needs: their name and number.

You can refuse this permission and everything else still works.

Reminders

Follow-up reminders are set on your own phone. Allowing notifications sends nothing anywhere.

Checking the app is genuine

The app uses Google Play Integrity so our database only answers the real Saral CRM app. Google checks the app and the device; this is not used to identify you or to track you.

Nothing else

The app does not collect your location, does not read your messages or call history, does not use your camera or microphone, and contains no advertising and no analytics about your behaviour.

Where it is kept

Information is stored using Google Firebase (Firestore and Authentication), operated by Google. It is protected in transit and at rest, and access rules make sure a business can only ever read its own data, never another business's.

Who can see your data

  • The people in your own business. The owner chooses how much employees can see.
  • Us, only when we need to: fixing a fault you have reported, or when the law requires it.
  • Nobody else. We do not sell it or pass it to anyone for marketing.

How long we keep it

As long as your business is using the app.

When an owner closes their business in the app, everything belonging to it (customers, timelines, follow-ups, messages and team records) is deleted permanently, straight away. It cannot be recovered.

When you delete your own account, your login and profile are removed. If colleagues remain, the business and its customers continue without you. How to delete your account.

What you can ask for

  • A copy of what we hold about you
  • A correction to anything that is wrong
  • Deletion of your account and your data
  • To stop us processing it

Deleting your account you can do yourself in the app, under Profile. For anything else, email us and we will answer within 30 days.

Children

The app is for businesses and is not intended for anyone under 18.

Changes to this policy

If we change it, we will update the date at the top of this page. For a change that matters, we will also tell business owners by email or in the app before it takes effect.
